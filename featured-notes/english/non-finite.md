# 非谓语动词三分法

非谓语动词不作句子谓语，不受人称和数限制，却是考试与写作的高频难点。这篇笔记用三分法帮你快速判断该用 to do、doing 还是 done。

## 一、基础概念

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">非谓语动词包括三类：不定式 to do、动名词 doing、分词（现在分词 doing 与过去分词 done）。它们在句中可作主语、宾语、定语、状语和补语。</span>

## 二、核心重点：三类形式的意义

- <span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">不定式 to do：表目的、将来或某次具体动作 —— To learn English well, you need practice. / I have a lot of work to do.</span>
- 动名词 doing：表习惯、抽象或一般行为，常作主语、宾语 —— Swimming is good exercise. / I enjoy reading.
- 现在分词 doing：表主动、进行 —— The girl standing there is my sister.
- 过去分词 done：表被动、完成 —— The book written by him is popular.

### 1. 接不定式还是动名词

- 常接 to do：want, decide, hope, plan, refuse（He decided to leave.）
- 常接 doing：enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest（She avoided answering.）
- 两者皆有但意义不同：remember / forget / stop / regret
- 再如：expect to do（期待做）、can't help doing（忍不住做）、have difficulty (in) doing（做某事有困难），如 I couldn't help laughing.

## 三、分析思路

<span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">判断三步：① 看是主动还是被动；② 看动作与谓语的时间关系（先发生、同时、后发生）；③ 选形式：主动且将来用 to do，主动且进行用 doing，被动且完成用 done。</span>

### 1. 意义对比示例

- Remember to lock the door when you leave.（记得去锁门，还没锁）
- I remember locking the door.（记得锁过门，已锁）
- He stopped to smoke.（停下来去抽烟）
- He stopped smoking.（停止抽烟）

## 四、易错提醒

- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错一：悬垂分词的逻辑主语必须与句子主语一致。错：Walking down the street, the trees looked beautiful. 对：Walking down the street, I found the trees beautiful.</span>
- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错二：独立主格结构中分词自带主语，如 The weather being fine, we went out；不要把 doing 与 done 的主动被动关系弄反。</span>

## 五、自测与小结

用所给动词的适当形式填空：

1. I look forward to ___ (hear) from you. —— hearing
2. The bridge ___ (build) last year is still in use. —— built
3. He got up early ___ (catch) the first train. —— to catch

<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">小结：非谓语只需抓住「主动/被动」和「时间关系」两个维度，形式自然就选对了。</span>
