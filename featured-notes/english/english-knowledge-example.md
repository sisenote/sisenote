# 定语从句关系词的选择

定语从句是英语语法的重点，也是考试高频考点。这篇笔记把关系词的选择规则整理成可复习的知识卡，并标出最容易出错的地方。

## 一、基础概念

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">定语从句：修饰名词或代词的从句，被修饰的词叫先行词，引导从句的词叫关系词。</span>

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">关系代词：who, whom, whose, which, that；关系副词：when, where, why。</span>

## 二、核心重点

<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">重点一：关系词的选择取决于先行词「指人还是指物」以及它在从句中「作什么成分」。</span>

<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">重点二：先行词指人用 who / whom / that，指物用 which / that，表所属关系用 whose。</span>

## 三、分析思路

<span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">解题两步：① 看先行词指人还是指物；② 看从句缺什么成分 —— 缺主语或宾语用关系代词，成分完整用关系副词。</span>

### 1. 关系代词用法

- <span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">who 指人，在从句中作主语</span>
- <span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">whom 指人，作宾语；whose 表所属关系</span>
- 例：The man <span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">who</span> spoke is our teacher.

### 2. 关系副词用法

- <span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">when 表时间，where 表地点，why 表原因</span>
- 例：This is the house <span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">where</span> I was born.

## 四、易错提醒

- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错一：先行词是时间 / 地点，但从句缺主语或宾语时，仍要用关系代词而非 when / where</span>
- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错二：that 不能引导非限制性定语从句</span>
- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">待记：关系词作宾语时常常可以省略，但作主语时不能省</span>

## 五、自测小题

填入合适的关系词：

1. The book ___ I bought yesterday is interesting. —— <span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">that / which（可省略）</span>
2. The day ___ we met was rainy. —— <span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">when</span>
3. The boy ___ father is a doctor is my friend. —— <span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">whose</span>

## 六、总结

判断关系词的顺序是<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">先看先行词、再看从句成分</span>。把这条思路固定下来，再配合易错点反复自测，就能稳定做对这类题。
