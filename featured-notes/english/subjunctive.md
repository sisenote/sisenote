# 虚拟语气的三种核心用法

虚拟语气表达说话人的愿望、假设或推测，谓语要用特殊形式。这类题失分率高，但只要按「时间 + 语境」两步判断，就能稳定做对。

## 一、基础概念

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">虚拟语气表示与事实相反、可能性很小或主观期望的情况，谓语动词不按常规时态变化，而要用「后退一步」的特殊形式。</span>

## 二、核心重点：if 条件句的三种时间

| 时间 | if 从句谓语 | 主句谓语 |
|---|---|---|
| 与现在相反 | 过去式（be 用 were） | would / could / might + 动词原形 |
| 与过去相反 | had + 过去分词 | would / could / might + have done |
| 与将来相反 | should do / were to do / 过去式 | would / could / might + 动词原形 |

<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">重点：If I were you, I would take the job. / If he had studied harder, he would have passed the exam.</span>

### 1. 三种时间真实例句

- 与现在相反：If I knew the answer, I would tell you.
- 与过去相反：If she had left earlier, she would not have missed the train.
- 与将来相反：If it should rain tomorrow, we would cancel the trip.

## 三、分析思路

<span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">判断三步：① 识别是不是假设、愿望或建议语境；② 确定与现在、过去还是将来相反；③ 按对应形式套用谓语。</span>

### 1. wish 从句

- I wish I knew her.（与现在相反，用过去式）
- I wish I had known her earlier.（与过去相反，用 had done）
- It's high time that we took action.（早该采取行动了，也用过去式表虚拟）

### 2. 建议类名词性从句

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">suggest / advise / insist / require / order 等后的 that 从句，谓语用 (should +) 动词原形。</span>

- The doctor suggested that he (should) quit smoking.
- It is important that we (should) protect the environment.

## 四、易错提醒

- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错一：混合时间虚拟要各自按时间处理，如 If you had followed my advice, you would be fine now（从句指过去、主句指现在）。</span>
- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错二：suggest 表「暗示」、insist 表「坚持认为」时不用虚拟，如 His pale face suggested that he was ill；此外正式文体中 be 动词一律用 were，不用 was。</span>

## 五、自测与小结

用所给动词的适当形式填空：

1. If I ___ (be) you, I would accept the offer. —— were
2. If he ___ (study) harder, he would have passed. —— had studied
3. He suggested that we ___ (start) at once. —— (should) start

<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">小结：虚拟语气的核心就是「时间与形式对应」，先定时间，再选形式，万变不离其宗。</span>
