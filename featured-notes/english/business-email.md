# 商务英语邮件写作指南

商务邮件讲究清晰、简洁、礼貌、专业。掌握固定结构和得体句型，既能把事情说清楚，也能给对方留下专业印象。

## 一、基础概念

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">商务邮件的典型结构是：主题行 + 称呼 + 开头表明目的 + 正文说明细节 + 结尾期待回复 + 落款。每一部分都有常用表达可套用。</span>

## 二、核心重点：结构与常用句型

- 主题行简明扼要：Subject: Meeting Arrangement for Next Week
- 称呼：Dear Mr. Smith / Dear Ms. Chen / Dear Team；不确定可用 Dear Sir or Madam
- 开头表明目的：<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">I am writing to inquire about... / I am writing to confirm our meeting...</span>
- 正文说明细节：Could you please...? / We would appreciate it if you could...
- 结尾期待回复：I look forward to your reply. / Thank you for your time and consideration.
- 落款：Best regards, / Sincerely,

### 1. 常见场景句型

- 请求：Would you mind sending me the file by Friday?
- 道歉：I apologize for the inconvenience caused.
- 感谢：Thank you for your prompt response.
- 跟进：I am following up on my previous email.
- 询问：I am writing to inquire about your summer course.
- 确认：I am writing to confirm our meeting on Friday morning.

<span class="sn-blue" data-sisenote-color="blue">语气要点：would、could、appreciate 比 will、can、want 更委婉得体，是商务邮件礼貌度的基础。</span>

正文不宜过长，一段只讲一件事；涉及多个事项时用 First、Also、Finally 分点列出，方便对方逐条回复。

## 三、分析思路

<span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">写邮件四步：① 写清主题行；② 开头一句说明来信目的；③ 正文分点陈述（用 first, also, finally 或项目符号）；④ 结尾写明期待与感谢，附上落款。</span>

写完后务必检查三件事：主题行是否准确概括邮件内容，称呼与落款是否完整，语气是否礼貌得体。一个小错误就可能影响对方对专业度的判断。

## 四、易错提醒

- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错一：语气过于生硬，把 Give me the report 改为 Could you please send me the report；请求用 would/could 比 will/can 更礼貌。</span>
- <span class="sn-yellow" data-sisenote-color="yellow">易错二：正式邮件避免缩写，用 cannot、do not 而非 can't、don't；日期格式要统一（英式 21 Nov 2024 或美式 Nov 21, 2024），别漏掉主题行和落款。</span>

## 五、自测与小结

补全一句「请求延期提交报告」的邮件开头：I am writing to ___ (request) an extension for the report submission.

> <span class="sn-green" data-sisenote-color="green">参考：I am writing to request an extension for the report submission. 后续可加 Could you please allow me two more days?</span>

<span class="sn-red" data-sisenote-color="red">小结：商务邮件 = 清楚的结构 + 礼貌的句型 + 统一的格式，三者缺一不可。</span>
